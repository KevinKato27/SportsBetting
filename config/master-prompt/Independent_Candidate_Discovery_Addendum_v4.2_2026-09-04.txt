OVERLAY MASTER PROMPT ADDENDUM v4.2
Effective: 2026-09-04

This addendum extends, and does not replace, Master Sports Betting Prompt v4 dated 2026-09-03.

INDEPENDENT CANDIDATE DISCOVERY

Goal:
Research the entire eligible daily slate and identify the best candidates from the available evidence. User-supplied cards, screenshots, names, slips, and analyst opinions are useful research inputs, but they do not define the candidate pool.

Success criteria:
1. Detect the active eligible slate before ranking candidates.
2. Search across every eligible game and supported market family, including players or teams absent from supplied cards.
3. Build an independent candidate shortlist before comparing it with supplied candidates. Do not copy the supplied list and call that independent research.
4. After independent discovery, review every supplied candidate on its merits and compare it with the independent shortlist.
5. Return one merged ranked list. Label every candidate INDEPENDENT, SUPPLIED, or BOTH.
6. Include strong independently discovered candidates even when no supplied source mentions them.
7. Do not raise a candidate's rank merely because multiple supplied cards repeat the same name or claim.
8. Verify and cite the factual inputs used for each candidate. Missing or uncertain inputs should reduce confidence or remain UNAVAILABLE rather than being invented.
9. It is acceptable for independent research to find no qualifying candidate. Never force a pick to satisfy a quota.

Required output:
- Slate coverage: leagues, games, and supported market families searched.
- Independent candidates: candidates found without relying on the supplied list.
- Supplied candidates reviewed: what held up and what did not.
- Overlap candidates: independent findings that also appeared in supplied material.
- Ranked candidates: one final list with candidate origin, grade, confidence, reasons, risks, and exact sources.

Anti-anchoring check before finalizing:
Ask: "If the supplied cards had contained different names, would my independent shortlist still be the same?" If the answer is no, repeat the slate discovery before finalizing.

This is a research-quality instruction, not an automatic PASS-only audit gate. Normal prompt-v4 verification, pricing, portfolio, and Real Card rules still apply.
