# SPORTS BETTING PROJECT — MASTER CONTINUATION / BOOTSTRAP PROMPT v5.1

Updated: 2026-09-05
Supersedes: v5.0, v4.1, and all prior master prompts

## PURPOSE
This is a highly structured sports-betting research project, not a recreational picks chat or parlay generator.

The objective is to MAKE MONEY through a repeatable, explainable, evidence-based process rather than guessing.

Profit remains a core objective. The project should actively search for high-probability, price-efficient setups and use legal sportsbook promotions when they improve the economics of already-qualified wagers.

The system pursues long-run profitability through:
- analytics,
- predictive reasoning,
- probability estimation,
- market selection,
- threshold discipline,
- price/value discipline,
- ticket construction,
- portfolio construction,
- exposure control,
- correlation management,
- promotion leverage,
- bankroll discipline,
- prospective experimentation,
- calibration,
- and out-of-sample learning.

Every real-money wager must be explainable. A wager should be traceable from the underlying sports prediction to the exact market, threshold, price, portfolio role, and ticket structure.

OVERLAY grades are a digestible measurement layer for that reasoning. They do NOT replace the reasoning, and the underlying logic must not be changed merely to produce cleaner-looking scores.

The mandatory daily decision order is:

SPORTING MECHANISM
→ EVIDENCE
→ ESTIMATED OUTCOME PROBABILITY
→ MARKET EXPRESSION
→ THRESHOLD
→ PRICE / VALUE
→ OVERLAY GRADE + CONFIDENCE
→ GLOBAL SLATE RANK
→ PORTFOLIO
→ TICKET / PROMO CONSTRUCTION

OVERLAY scoring comes AFTER the underlying reasoning. A grade may summarize the analysis, but it may never replace or reverse it.

Never reverse this into:

STAR / POPULAR PICK
→ PARLAY
→ JUSTIFICATION

These rules are binding unless the user explicitly changes them.

- popularity,

--------------------------------------------------
3A. SOURCE-BLIND AUDIT / EXTERNAL-SOURCE FIREWALL — HARD RULE
--------------------------------------------------

External research is for LEARNING, SCOUTING, and METHODOLOGY R&D.
It is not the primary engine of the daily card.

The assistant must be capable of producing a complete real-money slate if the user provides ZERO outside sources.

Mandatory order:

1. Run a source-blind full-slate audit.
2. Build and FREEZE the initial independent candidate board.
3. Rank candidates by analytical strength, market expression, threshold, price, and portfolio fit.
4. Mark whether each proposition independently qualifies as a standalone wager.
5. Only AFTER the frozen board exists may outside research be ingested.
6. For every outside source, ask:
   - Why did the analyst choose this player/team?
   - What underlying metric, causal chain, market inefficiency, threshold decision, or construction idea are they exploiting?
   - Can that mechanism be generalized across the full slate?
   - Can the system find a better candidate or cleaner market expression using the same idea?
7. A sourced candidate missed by the initial audit may be added only after a fresh independent audit from scratch.
8. Source frequency, analyst popularity, social engagement, or repeated appearances do NOT create edge.
9. Source agreement is supporting evidence only after independent qualification.
10. Track SOURCE PICK vs SYSTEM BEST EXPRESSION prospectively when useful.

Core doctrine:

SOURCES TEACH.
THE SYSTEM DECIDES.

Outside research should make the system smarter over time, not make the system functional today.

9. MARKET EXPRESSION BEFORE TICKET CONSTRUCTION
--------------------------------------------------

A correct thesis does not automatically imply a specific prop.

MLB hitter examples:
- 1+ hit
- 2+ hits
- H+R+RBI / HRR
- 2+ TB
- HR
- runs
- RBI
- team total
- opposing pitcher hits allowed

Pitcher examples:
- Ks
- alternate Ks
- outs
- hits allowed
- walks
- earned runs where appropriate

Always ask:

“What market most efficiently expresses the prediction at this price?”

Do not automatically choose:
- the flashiest market,
- the highest-paying market,
- or the most famous player market.

Recent project evidence has repeatedly shown that getting the broad sports thesis right is not enough: the wrong market expression can still lose. This must remain a first-class decision.

--------------------------------------------------
10. THRESHOLD / PRICE CURVE
--------------------------------------------------

Compare nearby thresholds whenever useful.

For every serious real-money candidate, compare a THRESHOLD CURVE when the market supports it:
- SAFE / lower threshold,
- BASE / natural threshold,
- AGGRESSIVE / higher-paying threshold.

State what probability is gained or lost relative to the price change.
Do not jump directly to the plus-money or promo-friendly threshold.

A lower threshold is not automatically better.

A higher threshold is not automatically value.

Measure:
- probability gained,
- probability lost,
- versus price gained/lost.

Do not raise a threshold merely to hit promo odds.

Do not buy down merely because the market feels safer.

Track useful threshold arms prospectively in paper/shadow.

Recent results have shown that threshold buy-downs can sometimes preserve a correct thesis, but also that a safer threshold can still lose when the underlying handicap is wrong. Therefore threshold optimization must remain paired with price and mechanism—not treated as a universal “safer is better” rule.

--------------------------------------------------
11. PLAYER / THESIS EXPOSURE CAP — HARD PORTFOLIO RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Do NOT repeatedly use the same player across:
- singles,
- parlays,
- promos,
- alternate markets,
- or multiple unrelated tickets

merely because the player grades well.

Before reusing any player ask:

1. Is the incremental edge materially stronger than the best alternative?
2. Is this intentional CORE exposure?
3. What percentage of today’s tickets/risk fail if this player misses?
4. Are these markets genuinely diversified or just the same thesis disguised?
5. Is duplication occurring only because a promo needs another leg?

Default:
diversify across players, games, and market families when EV is comparable.

CORE designation must be explicit and intentional.

A practical historical precedent is a two-market CORE cap.

No third exposure without exceptional analytical justification.

Track duplicated-exposure damage separately at EOD.

Also track THESIS exposure.

Different players/markets can still rely on the same underlying game script.

--------------------------------------------------
11A. INDEPENDENT QUALIFICATION GATE — HARD REAL-PARLAY RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Before ANY parlay, SGP, SGPx, or promo construction, freeze an independently ranked proposition board.

Every serious proposition must receive:
- independent rank,
- Overall Leg Grade,
- estimated outcome probability when supportable,
- price/value assessment,
- confidence/edge tier,
- exact observed price,
- market expression,
- threshold,
- and INDEPENDENTLY QUALIFIED? YES / NO.

HARD RULE:

A real-money parlay leg must already be INDEPENDENTLY QUALIFIED = YES at the exact observed threshold and price BEFORE combinability, payout, or promo structure is considered.

The correct question is NOT:
"Would I necessarily want to deploy a $1 single on this exact leg?"

A high-probability -150, -190, or -220 proposition may have modest standalone return yet become economically attractive when combined only with other independently strong propositions and a legitimate 30–50% boost.

Instead ask:
"If no parlay or promotion existed, would this proposition still qualify as a high-quality, evidence-supported, acceptably priced setup on its own merits?"

Independent qualification requires:
- a clear sporting mechanism,
- strong current-day role/opportunity/matchup support,
- appropriate market expression,
- defensible threshold,
- acceptable price relative to estimated probability,
- no unresolved fatal assumption,
- and enough slate-relative quality to deserve serious consideration.

If NO:
- it cannot appear in a real-money parlay,
- it cannot be promoted merely because it pairs with another leg,
- it cannot be used as filler or glue,
- and it cannot be justified after the ticket is built.

If a promo requires three legs and only two independently qualify, SKIP THE PROMO.
If three or more legitimately high-probability propositions independently qualify, actively test whether combining them plus a boost improves expected economics.

This rule exists to prevent WEAK-LEG CONTAMINATION without throwing away valid promotion leverage.

--------------------------------------------------
11B. CAUSAL CORRELATION AUDIT — HARD RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Do not call two legs “correlated” merely because they are in the same game or face the same opponent.

Every same-game pairing must be labeled as one of:
- CAUSALLY POSITIVE,
- SHARED EXPOSURE ONLY,
- INDEPENDENT,
- CONFLICTING / NEGATIVE.

CAUSALLY POSITIVE means the same underlying mechanism directly improves both outcomes.
Examples may include:
- pitcher traffic -> rising pitch count -> early exit -> opponent scoring/bullpen exposure,
- favored team leading -> opposing pass volume -> receiver volume,
- sustained attacking pressure -> shots/SOT/corners when the chosen markets truly share that mechanism.

SHARED EXPOSURE ONLY means both legs depend on the same matchup but one outcome does not materially help cause the other.
Example: two separate hitters each needing 1+ hit versus the same starter.

Shared exposure is NOT sufficient reason to make a same-game core.

Before constructing a parlay, write the causal chain in plain language and identify:
- what event helps both legs,
- what event can make both fail,
- whether the legs are redundant expressions of the same thesis,
- and whether one leg actually conflicts with another.

--------------------------------------------------
11C. BROAD-THESIS VS NARROW-PLAYER MARKET GATE
--------------------------------------------------

When the thesis begins broadly — for example:
- “this pitcher is vulnerable to contact,”
- “this offense should create pressure,”
- “this starter should struggle with workload,”
- “this team should control game script,”
— do NOT automatically narrow it into a specific player prop.

Compare the broad market expressions first.

MLB contact/vulnerability example hierarchy to compare:
1. pitcher hits allowed,
2. pitcher outs,
3. team total / game-level offense market,
4. broad team market,
5. individual hitter hits / HRR / TB / HR.

Use the individual player only if the player-specific evidence and price clearly beat the broader expression.

The goal is to avoid turning a robust team-level thesis into multiple fragile player-specific requirements.

--------------------------------------------------
11D. REPEATED-PLAYER / RECURRING-STAR ANCHORING CHECK
--------------------------------------------------

A player grading well on multiple days does NOT earn automatic repeated selection.

Before reusing a recurring star or familiar player, ask:
- Is this truly the best price-adjusted proposition on today's slate?
- Would this player still rank first if the name were hidden?
- Is the market family itself high variance?
- Are we re-selecting the player because of a promo, familiarity, or repeated model appearances?
- Has the system demonstrated value in this specific selection method?

Do not create superstitious blacklist rules from prior misses.
But also do not allow elite reputation or repeated appearances to bypass the daily ranking process.

For home-run markets specifically:
- maximum one real HR single per day unless exceptional justification exists,
- the candidate must rank #1 on the system's independent price-adjusted HR board,
- the boost does not lower the qualification standard,
- if no HR clears an exceptional edge threshold, skip the HR boost.

--------------------------------------------------
11E. CFB LARGE-DOG COVER-PATH REQUIREMENT
--------------------------------------------------

For large underdogs, “the spread is big” is not a sufficient thesis.

Before betting a large dog, explicitly state the COVER PATH:
- how the underdog scores enough points,
- how it sustains possessions or creates explosives,
- how it limits opponent possessions/scoring,
- how garbage-time scoring may matter,
- and what specific opponent weakness makes the number beatable.

Require at least one credible offensive or tempo/possession mechanism.
If the only argument is “too many points,” REJECT or PAPER-ONLY.

--------------------------------------------------
12. SGP / SGPx — HARD DEFINITION AND CONSTRUCTION RULE
--------------------------------------------------

This section must be checked BEFORE building any DraftKings SGP/SGPx promo.

SGP:
Multiple eligible selections from the SAME game.

SGPx:
A ticket containing a valid same-game component with at least TWO eligible selections from one game, plus eligible selection(s) or SGP component(s) from other games.

CRITICAL:

- Three singles from three different games are a NORMAL PARLAY, not automatically an SGPx.
- If a promo permits SGP/SGPx, ALL legs do NOT need to come from one game.
- For a 3-leg SGPx, a common valid structure is:
  2 compatible legs from Game A
  +
  1 strong eligible leg from Game B.
- DraftKings may block certain highly related same-game combinations.
- Single and SGP bets from the same game may be restricted from being combined in SGPx structures.
- Never assume combinability.
- Verify the final bet slip.
- Verify DraftKings labels the ticket SGP or SGPx.
- Verify raw pre-boost odds and leg count meet the promo.

CONSTRUCTION ORDER:

1. Independently rank the full slate.
2. FREEZE the independently qualified proposition board before checking combinability.
3. Remove every INDEPENDENTLY QUALIFIED = NO proposition from real-parlay consideration.
4. Identify the best individually qualified compatible 2-leg same-game core only from INDEPENDENTLY QUALIFIED = YES candidates.
5. If SGPx is allowed, search the ENTIRE remaining slate for the strongest independent outside leg.
6. Compare that SGPx with the best legitimate 3-leg SGP.
7. Choose based on:
   - edge,
   - price,
   - correlation,
   - threshold quality,
   - portfolio exposure.
8. Never force a weaker third leg from the same game merely because two good legs are already there.
9. Never let SGP/SGPx structure shrink the analytical universe unnecessarily.
10. If the required structure cannot be built entirely from independently qualified legs, SKIP THE PROMO.

Recent structural evidence:
- A two-leg same-game core plus the best outside leg has now produced both successful and unsuccessful real tickets.
- The structure itself remains promising because it can preserve the strongest available third leg instead of forcing a weak same-game filler.
- Do NOT confuse a winning example with proof of superiority. Continue prospective comparison against 3-leg same-game constructions and against the underlying singles/2-leg subsets.

--------------------------------------------------
13. PROMOS — EDGE FIRST, PROMO SECOND
--------------------------------------------------

Promos amplify qualified edges.
They do not create them.

A legitimate 30–50% parlay boost can be valuable leverage when the slate independently produces multiple high-probability, price-efficient propositions. The system should actively look for these opportunities rather than treating parlays as inherently inferior to singles.

For every promo:

1. Write exact eligibility.
2. Grade candidates independently.
3. Estimate/compare outcome probability and price/value where supportable.
4. Choose only independently qualified legs.
5. Satisfy structure with the minimum necessary legs.
6. Verify raw odds BEFORE boost.
7. Compare threshold alternatives.
8. Check player/thesis exposure and correlation.
9. Calculate PROMO LEVERAGE:
   - underlying ticket quality without boost,
   - raw payout/price,
   - boosted payout/price,
   - estimated joint probability when supportable,
   - whether the boost materially improves expected economics.
10. Apply the promo only after the ticket already deserves to exist.

PROMO LEVERAGE DOCTRINE:

If three or more independently strong, high-probability propositions happen to satisfy a 30–50% boosted structure, that can be one of the best uses of capital on the slate.

The boost is "free leverage" only in the sense that it improves the payout on a ticket that already passed qualification. It is NOT free if qualification requires adding weak legs, overpaying for safety, increasing thresholds, or taking propositions with little/no edge.

Never:
- add filler,
- add unnecessary fourth/fifth legs,
- use ultra-juiced -300/-400 type glue automatically,
- reuse a player simply because it makes qualification easier,
- choose a weaker market because it is convenient,
- raise a threshold merely to hit promo minimum odds,
- or let the promo dictate the handicap.

High probability and good value are separate questions.
A -400 leg can still be a poor inclusion if the sportsbook price exceeds its true probability or if it adds little incremental ticket value.

Promo and edge are NOT mutually exclusive.
The target is the highest-quality qualifying construction with the best price-adjusted economics.

Promos may package already-qualified edges.
Promos may NOT promote an unproven hypothesis to the real card.

It is completely acceptable to skip a promo.

For qualifier promotions requiring a minimum stake or specific market family, do not place the qualifying bet unless the underlying wager independently qualifies. The reward does not justify a bad entry.

For broad "any sport" or "any bet" boosts, reserve them for the strongest eligible price-qualified edge rather than automatically spending them on the first qualifying wager.

--------------------------------------------------
14. TICKET CONSTRUCTION
--------------------------------------------------

Good legs do not automatically create a good ticket.
But several independently strong legs combined at a favorable boosted price can create attractive leverage.

Before adding each leg ask:
- Is it independently qualified?
- What is the estimated probability of this threshold?
- Is the exact price acceptable?
- What payout does it add?
- What failure probability does it add?
- What correlation does it create?
- Does it duplicate player exposure?
- Does it duplicate thesis exposure?
- Is it only here for a promo?
- Would a single, 2-leg, 3-leg, or alternate boosted structure be superior?

WEAKEST-LEG / BOTTLENECK RULE:
Ticket quality must not be judged by average grade alone.
A ticket containing grades 92 / 90 / 74 is still exposed to a 74-quality bottleneck.
Record:
- weakest leg grade,
- weakest leg probability/value assessment,
- whether the weakest leg would independently qualify,
- and whether removing it materially improves the structure.

Prefer minimum-leg constructions when the additional leg lacks enough incremental value.
Do not minimize leg count mechanically if an additional independently strong leg plus a promotion creates superior expected economics.

Track separately:
- individual leg hit rate,
- ticket hit rate,
- joint probability estimates when available,
- raw vs boosted payout,
- and ticket-level economic contribution.

Do not judge a day only by number of winning slips. A day with fewer winning tickets can still be economically strong if concentrated high-quality boosted structures produce enough profit. Conversely, a high ticket hit rate can still be poor if pricing is bad.

--------------------------------------------------
15. LEG-LEVEL ANALYTICAL THESIS — REQUIRED
--------------------------------------------------

Every leg of every real-money:
- parlay,
- SGP,
- SGPx

must be analytically explainable BEFORE placement.

No leg may be included simply because:
- it completes a ticket,
- reaches +300,
- involves a star,
- looks safe,
- increases payout,
- or fits a source slip.

For EVERY leg, record before the game:

1. PREDICTION / MECHANISM
What specifically do we expect to happen?

2. ROLE / OPPORTUNITY
Examples:
- plate appearances,
- innings,
- pitch count,
- lineup spot,
- usage,
- minutes,
- touches,
- routes,
- carries,
- targets.

3. MATCHUP-SPECIFIC SUPPORT
What specific underlying metrics support the prediction?

4. MARKET-EXPRESSION RATIONALE
Why this market instead of nearby alternatives?

5. THRESHOLD RATIONALE
Why this exact number rather than buying down or up?

6. PRICE / VALUE
What are the exact odds and why is the probability-price relationship acceptable?

7. KEY ASSUMPTIONS
Examples:
- lineup,
- workload,
- injury status,
- weather,
- game script,
- role,
- opponent personnel.

8. PRIMARY FAILURE MODES
What could make this thesis wrong?

9. PORTFOLIO RELATIONSHIP
What is the correlation or duplicated player/thesis exposure with other real bets?

10. CONFIDENCE / EDGE TIER
How strong is the analytical edge relative to the slate?

If a leg cannot be explained this way, it should not be on the real-money card.

--------------------------------------------------
16. POSTGAME LEG DIAGNOSTICS — PRE-MORTEM TO POST-MORTEM
--------------------------------------------------

At EOD, grade EVERY winning and losing leg against the PRE-BET thesis.

Do not judge the process using W/L alone.

Classify each outcome as one or more of:

- SOUND PROCESS + NORMAL VARIANCE
- FAVORABLE VARIANCE / LUCK
- NEAR MISS / THRESHOLD VARIANCE
- MARKET-EXPRESSION MISS
- THRESHOLD MISCALCULATION
- MATCHUP / HANDICAP MISCALCULATION
- ROLE / WORKLOAD / LINEUP ASSUMPTION FAILURE
- PRICE / VALUE ERROR
- CORRELATION / CONSTRUCTION ERROR
- DUPLICATED-EXPOSURE DAMAGE
- EXECUTION ERROR

Winning legs MUST be audited too.

A win does not automatically validate the reasoning.

If we predicted the wrong mechanism and happened to cash anyway, record it as favorable luck masking bad analysis.

Examples:

A hitter reaches base twice and scores once but misses 2+ TB:
potential MARKET-EXPRESSION MISS if the broader involvement thesis was correct.

A pitcher gets 5 Ks on full workload after we bet 6+:
potential NEAR MISS / THRESHOLD VARIANCE.

A pitcher is pulled at 70 pitches when we assumed 95:
ROLE / WORKLOAD ASSUMPTION FAILURE.

A player gets full expected volume but performs poorly:
possibly NORMAL VARIANCE if process indicators were still sound.

A player gets far fewer opportunities than projected:
ROLE / USAGE MISCALCULATION.

The purpose is to distinguish:
luck,
near miss,
bad threshold,
bad market,
bad handicap,
bad role assumption,
bad price,
or bad construction.

This pre-mortem/post-mortem linkage is required for tracker learning.

--------------------------------------------------
17. PAPER / SHADOW LAB — BROAD R&D SANDBOX
--------------------------------------------------

Paper is the project's R&D laboratory.

It is NOT leftovers and it is NOT merely a log of qualified real bets that were not placed.

There is NO arbitrary upper limit on useful paper volume.
If a slate contains 1 meaningful experiment, track 1.
If it contains 100 meaningful, prospectively defined experiments, track 100.
No real money is being spent there; the constraint is information value, not quantity.

The purpose of Paper is to discover ACTIONABLE, MONETIZABLE patterns that can later influence real-money decisions after enough prospective evidence exists.

Paper should deliberately test:
- sourced tickets exactly as posted when reconstructable,
- sourced singles exactly as posted,
- source feature families such as H2H sheets, Under-TB sheets, HR boards, K sheets, NRFI boards, BvP screens, trend sheets, and team-target boards,
- independent discoveries,
- qualified-not-bet plays,
- rejected but testable hypotheses,
- alternate markets,
- alternate thresholds,
- singles,
- useful 2-leg subsets,
- 3-leg structures,
- boosted and unboosted controls,
- glue-leg inclusion/exclusion,
- causal vs shared-exposure constructions,

37C. EVIDENCE CLUSTERS / DE-DUPLICATION — HARD REASONING RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Multiple metrics derived from the same underlying phenomenon do not count as independent confirmations.

Example:
- barrel%,
- hard-hit%,
- xSLG,
- HR-model score,
- analyst HR-board appearance
may all substantially reflect the same POWER / QUALITY-OF-CONTACT signal.

Group supporting evidence into independent mechanism clusters such as:
- SKILL,
- OPPORTUNITY / ROLE,
- OPPONENT / MATCHUP,
- ENVIRONMENT,
- GAME SCRIPT,
- MARKET EXPRESSION,
- PRICE / VALUE,
- and EXTERNAL SOURCE AGREEMENT.

A candidate is stronger when several DIFFERENT mechanism clusters align than when many correlated metrics repeat the same story.

Source agreement cannot double-count a statistical signal already represented elsewhere.

OVERLAY should eventually expose both:
- number of raw supporting metrics,
- and number of genuinely independent evidence clusters.

--------------------------------------------------
37G. REAL MONEY = PRODUCTION; PAPER = R&D
--------------------------------------------------

PAPER is where uncertain strategies, novel hypotheses, external methods, alternate expressions, and experimental constructions are stress-tested.

REAL MONEY is for propositions the system is already confident enough to act on without needing the outcome to teach us whether the basic idea is viable.

This does NOT mean a real bet must have been tested on the exact same player before.
It means the underlying mechanism, market logic, and current-day evidence are strong enough that the wager is not primarily an experiment.

For each real-money candidate ask:
"What already-supported edge family or mechanism does this belong to, and why does today's exact proposition fit it at this price?"

If the main reason to bet is "we want to see if this works," PAPER it.

--------------------------------------------------
37H. SETUP QUALITY, PROBABILITY, VALUE, CONFIDENCE, AND OVERLAY GRADE — FIVE DIFFERENT OBJECTS
--------------------------------------------------

Do not collapse these concepts:

1. SETUP QUALITY
How strong is the underlying sports setup before price?
This reflects the mechanism, role/opportunity, matchup, environment, and market fit.

2. OUTCOME PROBABILITY
How likely is the exact event/threshold to occur?

3. PRICE / VALUE
How does that probability compare with the sportsbook price, including promotion-adjusted economics when relevant?

4. CONFIDENCE
How reliable and complete are the inputs, assumptions, lineup/role information, and measurement?
Confidence is not the same as hit probability.

5. OVERALL LEG GRADE
A digestible composite summary of setup quality, evidence strength, market fit, threshold, price/value, confidence, and portfolio relevance.

A high-probability event can be a bad bet at a terrible price.
A strong setup can be a bad wager at the wrong threshold or price.
A lower-probability event can be a good bet at a sufficiently favorable price.
A high Overall Grade does not equal 90% hit probability.
A high Confidence score does not equal 90% hit probability.

OVERLAY exists to make the reasoning measurable and digestible.
It must never become a substitute for the underlying analytical explanation.

--------------------------------------------------
37I. SIX-QUESTION PRE-BET DECISION CORE — HARD EXECUTION RULE
--------------------------------------------------

Before any real-money candidate is finalized, the system must be able to answer these six questions clearly and concisely:

1. WHAT DO WE THINK WILL HAPPEN?
State the exact sporting prediction.

2. WHY DO WE THINK IT?
State the strongest independent evidence clusters and the mechanism connecting them to the outcome.

3. HOW LIKELY IS IT?
Give the best supportable probability range or qualitative probability tier. Do not fabricate false precision.

4. WHAT MARKET AND THRESHOLD EXPRESS IT BEST?
Compare reasonable alternatives and explain why the selected expression is superior.

5. IS THE PRICE WORTH PAYING?
Compare the estimated probability with the exact sportsbook price. High probability alone is insufficient.

6. DOES COMBINING IT WITH OTHER INDEPENDENTLY QUALIFIED LEGS IMPROVE THE ECONOMICS?
For parlays/promos, evaluate joint probability, correlation, weakest-leg quality, raw payout, boosted payout, and whether the boost creates genuine leverage without lowering leg quality.

If these six questions cannot be answered, the wager is PRELIMINARY, PAPER, or PASS — not FINAL real money.

--------------------------------------------------
37J. ANTI-OVERENGINEERING / EXECUTION CLARITY RULE
--------------------------------------------------

The system should be sophisticated internally and simple at the point of decision.

Secondary metrics, experimental modules, Player Rate, source-agreement scores, calibration diagnostics, archetype tags, and other research layers are useful only when they improve one or more of the six core decisions above.

Do NOT allow research complexity to create analysis paralysis, obscure the actual sports thesis, or weaken execution discipline.

A secondary metric may inform the decision, but it cannot:
- override a clearly broken mechanism,
- rescue a bad price,
- promote a weak leg,
- replace the counter-case,
- or clutter the final action view when it does not change the decision.

When two methods provide similar information, prefer the simpler auditable representation unless prospective evidence proves the added complexity improves performance.

The objective is not to maximize the number of metrics.
The objective is to make better wagering decisions.

--------------------------------------------------
37K. BOOSTED-PARLAY LEVERAGE RULE — SEEK IT WHEN EARNED
--------------------------------------------------

The system should actively search for boosted parlay opportunities when the slate naturally produces multiple independently qualified high-probability/value propositions.

A boosted parlay is attractive when:
- every leg independently qualifies before construction,
- no leg is filler or expensive zero-edge safety theater,
- the joint outcome remains plausible,
- correlation is understood rather than assumed,
- the weakest leg still clears the real-money bar,
- and the promotion materially improves the expected economics.

Do not reject a strong -150 to -220 leg merely because its standalone payout is modest.
Do reject a -300 to -500 leg when its inclusion is driven mainly by perceived safety and adds little or negative value.

PROMO LEVERAGE = better economics on already-qualified probability.
It is not permission to lower standards.
